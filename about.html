Your videos. Your pace.

Melo is a personal Android video and listening app designed around choosing what to play. A quiet home screen puts search and your own library first.

Less pulling you in

There is no Shorts feed. Shorts identified by the app are hidden from browsing and search results, while a deliberately pasted Shorts link can still open. Related-video auto-queue and the related panel start switched off.

A familiar place to return

Search YouTube, revisit your history, or open a saved playlist. Music and learning shortcuts run searches only when you tap them. Normal search results can still load more as you scroll.

Listening beyond the screen

The player supports video and background audio. Minimising defaults to background playback; device battery settings and playback availability can affect this behaviour.

Independent, with open-source roots

Melo is a personal fork of NewPipe v0.28.8, using NewPipe Extractor v0.26.3. NewPipe contributors built the underlying playback, extraction and much of the interface. Melo is not an official NewPipe or YouTube product.
