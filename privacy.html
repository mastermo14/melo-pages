Privacy, in plain language.

Prepared for Melo 0.1.2 on 12 November 2026. This describes the personal fork’s current source; review it if the app or hosting changes.

Requests needed to play

Searching, loading thumbnails and playing videos makes network requests to YouTube and related content servers through the underlying extraction and playback libraries. Those services receive technical request information, such as your IP address and the resources requested. Melo is not an anonymity tool.

Information on your device

The app can store preferences, subscriptions, saved playlists, search history and playback history locally. History controls are available in Settings. Downloads and exports are stored in the locations you choose; remove those files separately when needed.

Crash reports are your choice

The included crash handler prepares local error details. In this Melo revision, Copy report and Share report let you decide whether and where to send them. Reports may contain device details, app version, request URLs and comments you entered. Review them before sharing. There is no configured Melo support inbox, and reports are not automatically submitted to the NewPipe team.

Accounts and added services

This fork adds no Melo account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or Melo-operated backend. Third-party content requests still occur. External browser links and apps you choose for sharing have their own privacy practices.

These help pages

The app’s bundled About, Help and Privacy pages are read from local files and do not need a website connection. The matching static website has no added analytics, cookies, forms or third-party fonts. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes requests to serve the site under its own privacy practices.

Control and questions

Use history settings to manage stored activity. Clearing app data removes app-local records; downloaded or exported files may remain. For questions about a particular APK, contact the person who provided your Melo build. No public support address has been configured yet.
