A little help.

Simple answers for watching and listening with Melo.

How do I play a video?

Enter a search or paste a YouTube link into the home-screen search box, then tap Search. Choose a result to open the player.

Can I listen with the screen off?

Minimise Melo while playing. Background audio is the initial setting. You can also choose Background in the player. If playback stops, check Melo’s player settings, notification permission, and your phone’s battery restrictions.

Where are Shorts?

There is no swipeable Shorts feed. Shorts identified by the app are hidden from browsing and search. Paste a specific Shorts link when you intentionally want to watch one. A short ordinary video is not hidden just because of its length.

Will another video start automatically?

Related-video auto-queue starts off. A playlist or queue you choose can still continue playing. Review Player settings if you want to change this.

Does Melo remove every advertisement?

Melo uses NewPipe’s extraction-based playback rather than a full YouTube browser page. It does not promise permanent ad-free playback. Sponsor messages recorded inside a video remain part of that video.

Why is a video unavailable?

YouTube changes, temporary network restrictions, or private, paid, age-gated and region-restricted videos can prevent playback. Try another video and network. Some failures require an app update.

How do I report a problem?

On the error screen, use Copy report or Share report. Send it to the person maintaining your Melo build, with your phone model, Android version, and the action that failed. Review the report first: it may include video links, comments and device details. Melo does not send these reports to NewPipe’s support team.

Can I make the text larger?

Melo follows Android’s font-size setting. Choose the app theme in Settings → Appearance. Larger text may require more scrolling.

How do I update safely?

Use an APK with the same app ID and signing key as your installed version. If Android rejects an update, do not uninstall before exporting any playlists or history you want to keep. This build’s minimum Android version is Android 5; future builds may differ.
